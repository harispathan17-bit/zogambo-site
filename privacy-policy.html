1. Who We Are

Zogambo Studios LLP ("Zogambo," "we," "us," or "our") is an independent mobile gaming studio incorporated in India, engaged in the development and publishing of casual mobile games for iOS and Android platforms. Our registered office is located in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

This Privacy Policy applies to all products, services, mobile applications, and websites operated by us, including our website at zogambo.in and all mobile games we publish (collectively, the "Services").

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy is an electronic record structured to align with the Information Technology Act, 2000, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and international privacy frameworks governing global application distribution, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using any of our Services, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use immediately.

This policy covers how we collect, store, use, share, and protect your personal data — through our online Services (website and mobile apps) and any offline interactions such as email correspondence.

3. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and email address when you contact or correspond with us
  • Messages, feedback, and support requests sent to developer@zogambo.in
  • Any information you voluntarily share through our website or in-game features

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type, model, hardware specifications, and operating system version
  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) and persistent unique device identifiers
  • IP address and coarse geographic location (restricted to country and region level)
  • App version, installation source, system language, and session metadata
  • Gameplay telemetry data — levels completed, session duration, core feature usage, and in-game custom events
  • Technical crash logs and diagnostic app performance reports
  • In-app purchase histories handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store — we do not log or store payment card details

Information from Third-Party Partners

  • Attribution parameters from advertising networks pinpointing how you discovered our mobile games
  • Aggregated usage patterns and behavioral segments from third-party analytics integrations
  • Social Media Platform Data: If you utilize features connected to Facebook/Meta (such as login extensions, sharing metrics, or specific SDK targeting), the network may route device data and custom app events to us, based on your authorization settings on that network.

4. Why We Collect Your Information

  • Provide, operate, optimize, and maintain our games and website ecosystem
  • Personalise your gameplay experience and serve the correct localized app variants
  • Analyse technical performance, resolve active bugs, and inform future design updates
  • Respond to your support inquiries and process user feedback loops
  • Serve targeted or contextual in-game advertisements through ad mediation and network partners
  • Evaluate the efficiency of our mobile user acquisition and publishing campaigns
  • Detect, mitigate, and investigate malicious behaviors, cheating, and security updates
  • Comply with applicable local and global regulatory duties

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Game operation: Device and gameplay data is used to serve the correct app version and ensure a stable experience.
  • Analytics: Anonymised usage data helps us understand feature engagement and guides our product roadmap.
  • Advertising: Advertising IDs are used to serve and measure in-game ads. We do not link them to your personally identifiable information.
  • Communication: If you contact us, we use your email solely to respond. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.
  • Security: IP addresses and device data help detect anomalous activity and prevent fraud.
  • Legal compliance: We may retain data as required by the IT Act, 2000 and the DPDP Act, 2023.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their independent marketing purposes. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party providers who process data on our behalf under written data processing agreements. They may only use data to perform services for us.

Third-Party Services We Currently Use

  • Google AdMob & Google Firebase — Serving in-game advertisements, app analytics, and crash reporting
  • Unity Analytics & Gaming Services — Gameplay event tracking, session diagnostics, and performance optimization
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) — Measuring application installations, attribution mapping, and ad delivery optimization
  • AppsFlyer / Adjust — Mobile Measurement Partners (MMP) for secure user acquisition source tracking
  • AppLovin / IronSource / Global Publishing Squads — Specialized ad mediation network pipelines or publishing test suites

Legal Requirements

We may disclose data to government or law enforcement authorities if required by applicable law, a valid governmental request, or a lawful court order under the IT Act, 2000, the DPDP Act, 2023, or any other applicable statute.

Business Transfers

If Zogambo Studios LLP undergoes a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer to the successor entity. We will notify you where reasonably practicable.

7. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

Our games contain advertisements served by third-party ad networks. These networks may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, and device data to serve targeted or contextual ads. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

To limit interest-based advertising:

  • iOS (14+): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" (or "Reset/Delete Advertising ID")

Opting out does not remove ads from our games — it means the ads you see may be less relevant to your interests.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website at zogambo.in uses cookies to improve your experience:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality and cannot be disabled without affecting site performance
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand visitor behaviour, powered by Google Analytics

You may disable cookies in your browser settings. This may affect website features but will not affect your ability to use our mobile games.

9. Children's Privacy & Age Limits

Our mobile games are designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiers from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 for residents of the EU/EEA) without verifiable parental consent.

If we determine through structural app configurations or automated filters that an active player is below the legal threshold, we will either enforce sandbox boundaries (disabling behavioral ad mapping and social features) or scrub the records collected up to that point.

Users aged 13–18 should review this policy with a parent or guardian before using our Services.

If you believe we may have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us at developer@zogambo.in.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Gameplay and device data may be retained in anonymised or aggregated form for analytical purposes after original personal data has been deleted.

To request deletion of your data, email us at developer@zogambo.in with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

11. Your Rights

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to object: Object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, write to developer@zogambo.in. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request and will respond within 30 days.

11a. Specific Provisions for EEA and UK Users (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), your data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Zogambo Studios LLP acts as the Data Controller for your personal data.

Legal Bases for Processing

We rely on the following legal grounds to process your information:

  • Consent: Where you have given clear consent, specifically for serving personalized, interest-based advertisements and tracking cookies. You may withdraw this consent at any time.
  • Performance of a Contract: When processing is necessary to provide the Services, such as maintaining gameplay states, delivering app updates, or fulfilling in-app purchases.
  • Legitimate Interests: For our legitimate business goals, provided they do not override your privacy rights. This includes analyzing game performance, preventing fraud, security tracking, and optimization.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with binding regulatory or law enforcement demands.

International Data Transfers

Because Zogambo is located in India, your personal data will be transferred to and processed in countries outside the EEA and UK. To ensure your data remains protected, we rely on standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or equivalent legal safeguards implemented by our third-party infrastructure and SDK partners.

11b. Specific Provisions for California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

This section applies solely to California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, you have distinct rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of Information Collected and Disclosed

In the preceding 12 months, we have collected and disclosed to our third-party service providers (such as ad networks and analytics tools) the following categories of personal information for business purposes: Identifiers (IP addresses, Advertising IDs), Internet or electronic network activity data (gameplay metadata, crash logs), and Geolocation data (country/region level).

Notice of "Sale" or "Sharing" of Personal Information

We do not exchange your personal data for money. However, our use of third-party ad networks to serve targeted in-game ads is classified as "selling" or "sharing" under California law. You have the right to opt-out of this sharing.

To exercise your right to opt-out, please follow the device-level tracking restrictions outlined in Section 7 of this policy, or contact us at developer@zogambo.in with the subject "CCPA Do Not Sell/Share My Info".

12. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organisational security measures including data encryption, restricted access controls, firewalls, and regular security reviews.

No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data. You transmit information to us at your own risk. We recommend not sharing account credentials with anyone.

If you suspect any unauthorised access or a security incident involving our Services, please notify us immediately at developer@zogambo.in.
